## Clear the gutters

Blocked gutters push water behind the walls. Clear them once the leaves have fallen.

## Check the seals

Hold your hand near closed windows and doors on a windy day. If you feel a draught, the seals are worth replacing.

## Test the outside tap

Turn off the outside tap at the isolation valve and drain it, so that frost cannot split the pipe.
